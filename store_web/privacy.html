Hindu Calendar Privacy Policy

Effective: 27 September 2026

Hindu Calendar is designed as an offline-first calendar and Panchang utility. The core app does not require an account and does not contain advertising or behavioral analytics.

Local app data

Personal events, selected language, and selected city are stored locally on the user’s device. The app does not upload this calendar data to a developer-operated server.

Home-screen widgets

The app writes a limited calendar summary to Android widget storage on the same device so its optional home-screen widgets can display today’s Panchang and upcoming events.

Voluntary developer support

The app may offer an optional voluntary tip to the independent developer. Supporting the developer unlocks no digital feature, content, badge, or service. Payment is completed in an external UPI application chosen by the user. Hindu Calendar does not read or store banking credentials.

Physical handwritten thank-you note

A supporter may optionally request a physical handwritten thank-you note. If the user chooses this option, the app opens the user’s email client with a draft. The user decides whether to send a name, postal address, and optional transaction reference. Hindu Calendar itself does not store this information. Information received by the developer for this purpose is used only to mail the requested note and should be deleted within 30 days after dispatch.

Third-party services

The app uses Flutter framework components and local-device packages for preferences, widgets, and launching external apps. It does not include an advertising SDK or behavioral analytics SDK.

Panchang scope

The main calendar follows a North Indian Purnimanta profile. Regional traditions and other Panchang methods can produce different festival dates or Muhurta times.
