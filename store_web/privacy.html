Hindu Calendar Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 October 2026

Hindu Calendar is designed as an offline-first calendar and Panchang utility. The core app does not require an account and does not contain advertising or behavioral analytics.

Local app data

Personal events, selected language, selected city, and optionally saved location coordinates are stored locally on the user’s device. The app does not upload this calendar data to a developer-operated server.

Optional foreground location

“Use current location” requests approximate or precise foreground location only when chosen. Coordinates are saved locally for sunrise, sunset, moonrise and location-dependent Panchang rules. The app does not track location continuously or in the background and does not request location on every startup. Saved coordinates work offline. Manual city selection is always available. This release uses the India/IST calendar profile. No coordinates are sent to a developer server and no reverse geocoding is used. Android location services may use device-managed positioning services when obtaining a location.

Home-screen widgets

The app writes a limited calendar summary to Android widget storage on the same device so its optional home-screen widgets can display today’s Panchang and upcoming events.

Optional developer support

The app may offer an optional way to support the independent developer. Supporting the developer does not unlock or change any digital feature, content, badge, or service. Payment is completed in an external UPI application chosen by the user. Hindu Calendar does not read or store banking credentials, a UPI PIN, or payment details.

Mailed handwritten thank-you note

A supporter may optionally request a physical handwritten thank-you note. If the user chooses this option, the app opens the user’s email client with a draft. The user decides whether to send a name, postal address, and optional transaction reference. Hindu Calendar itself does not store this information. The developer will use postal-address information only to mail the requested note and delete it within 30 days after dispatch.

Third-party services

The app uses Flutter framework components and local-device packages for preferences, widgets, and launching external apps. It does not include an advertising SDK or behavioral analytics SDK.

Panchang scope

The main calendar follows a North Indian Purnimanta profile. Regional traditions and other Panchang methods can produce different festival dates or Muhurta times.

Optional district names and reminders

When you explicitly acquire or refresh your current location, Android's system Geocoder may use its provider to look up the district and state from your coordinates. The lookup may require internet. An offline bundled district lookup can resolve ambiguous division labels, including a one-time repair of previously saved names. We do not operate a location server. Coordinates and returned labels are saved on your device and reused offline; location is never tracked in the background.

Morning Panchang is enabled by default unless previously disabled; notification permission remains required and reminders can be disabled in the app. Personal-event reminders are separately optional. Notifications use locally calculated summaries and a short daily background renewal. No special alarm access is requested; Android may delay delivery after 5 AM. Notification content can appear on your lock screen according to your phone settings. No GPS acquisition or network calendar request is needed for reminders.
