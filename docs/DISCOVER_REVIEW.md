All 137 prior Hindi readings have been rewritten from the meaning rather than translated sentence by sentence from English. There are 18 additional referenced shastra/spiritual/patriotic readings, including 15 original Sanskrit passages. A source-linked original and an app-authored Hindi bhavarth are visibly distinct. Earlier teacher-specific prompts were rewritten too. Hindi references use Hindi text names. Total inventory: 155.

Buddhist/Jain comparative entries remain labelled in the source archive but are excluded from the daily philosophy pool. Patriotic readings focus on मातृभूमि, सेवा, एकता and कर्तव्य, grounded in the Atharva Veda, Rig Veda and Vivekananda's original works. They do not introduce invented slogans attributed to scripture.

Every card offers image sharing. The exact PNG is previewed before Android's system chooser opens. Its reading, source, current selected-location Panchang, Vikram Samvat, civil-day tithi sequence, app icon and Play Store QR form one measured layout. All tithi intervals fit, including days with three tithis. Text wraps naturally without clipping. No device storage permission or new package is required. Personal events and coordinates are absent from the shared artifact.

The renderer and sharing bridge are in `discovery_share_service.dart`, `discovery_share_preview.dart` and `DiscoveryImageShare.kt`. Existing PDF sharing and the three-section layout remain intact. See CHECKPOINT_33.md for validation and Android device-testing limitations.
