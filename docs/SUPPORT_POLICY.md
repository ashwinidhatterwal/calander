# Voluntary Developer Support Policy

The app must never:
- block functionality behind support;
- remove ads after support (there are no ads);
- grant a badge, theme, content, digital item or premium status for support;
- automatically open the support screen;
- show repeated donation/support nags;
- describe the developer tip as a tax-exempt charitable donation.

Current UI wording deliberately uses “support development” / “voluntary tip”. The user chooses the amount in an external UPI app. The app does not read the payment result.

A supporter may optionally request a **physical handwritten thank-you note**. The note is a courtesy and does not affect app functionality. Postal details are sent by the user through their email client, not stored by the app, and should be deleted by the developer within 30 days after dispatch.
