# Discover editorial inventory

137 bilingual records: 127 cultural, spiritual and teaching readings plus 10 dated history records. Build 32 adds 48 individually referenced readings. Sources: `flutter_app/lib/data/daily_discoveries.dart` and `wisdom_readings.dart`. Month numbers use Chaitra=1 through Phalguna=12; tithis use Shukla=1–15 and Krishna=16–30. Tithi matches suggest reading, not definitive festival dates. New entries are original paraphrases or story retellings; prompts are app-authored.
