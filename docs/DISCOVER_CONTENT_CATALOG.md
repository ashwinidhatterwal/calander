# Discover editorial inventory — build 33

155 bilingual records. All 137 earlier Hindi titles and bodies have been rewritten in natural Hindi. The 18 additions emphasize Sanatan scriptures, spiritual practice, and patriotic service. Fifteen contain original Sanskrit passages, with Hindi bhavarth kept distinct from the original text. Prompts are app-authored suggestions.

The daily experience remains three cards. Spiritual and philosophical sections prioritize Sanatan shastra, Bhakti, Yoga and related Indian ethical traditions. Six older Buddhist/Jain entries remain in this inventory for provenance but are not in the daily philosophy rotation. Dated historical and heritage facts remain in the third section.

Every card can be previewed and shared as a branded PNG with the day's selected-location Panchang. Exact references and source links are below.

Month numbers use Chaitra=1 through Phalguna=12; tithis use Shukla=1–15 and Krishna=16–30. A tithi reading suggestion is not a festival-date declaration. All entries are bundled offline.
