App name
Hindu Calendar

Short description
A clean Hindu calendar with Tithi, Panchang, festivals, vrats and your personal days.

Full description
Hindu Calendar is a Hindi-first, offline-first daily calendar that makes Panchang information easy to understand without turning the app into a dense reference table.

Highlights:
• Familiar monthly calendar with Tithi on every date
• Major festivals and vrats shown directly inside date tiles
• Detailed day Panchang: Tithi, Paksha, Maas, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana, sunrise/sunset, moonrise/moonset and useful daily periods
• North Indian Purnimanta month profile
• Hindi and English
• Location-aware daily timings
• Add birthdays, anniversaries, puja and family occasions
• Repeat personal events by either Gregorian date or Hindu Maas–Paksha–Tithi
• Android home-screen widgets
• No advertising
• No required account
• Personal events are stored on the device

The app is fully free. An optional voluntary developer-support option may be available under About; supporting never unlocks digital features.
